# SPARC Table 1 (galaxy sample)

`SPARC_Lelli2016c.mrt`, unchanged from the SPARC website
(https://astroweb.cwru.edu/SPARC/SPARC_Lelli2016c.mrt; Lelli, McGaugh &
Schombert 2016, AJ 152, 157). Per galaxy: Hubble type, distance and its
error and method, inclination and its error, 3.6 µm luminosity, effective
radius and surface brightness, disc scale length and central surface
brightness, HI mass and radius, flat rotation speed, quality flag and
rotation-curve references.

**Not exported to agents.** It contains the observed flat rotation speed
(Vflat) of every galaxy, which is an answer for the hidden galaxies of tests
01 and 02. No test lists this folder in DATA_DIRS. It is used only for
analyses (e.g. `analysis/rar_age.py`, per-galaxy distance and inclination
uncertainties).
